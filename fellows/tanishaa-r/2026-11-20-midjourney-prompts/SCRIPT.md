## Fact-check notes
- **Midjourney, Prompt Basics** (docs.midjourney.com, read 2026-11-20): "Short and simple prompts typically generate the best images"; "Avoid making long lists or detailed instructions; these can confuse the process"; the poppies example (long request vs "Colored pencil illustration of bright orange California poppies"); "Fewer details mean more variety, but you get less control over the outcome"; the seven areas (subject, medium, environment, lighting, color, mood, composition); describe what you want, the "no cake" example and the `--no` parameter.
- **Handbook §6.4:** the seven questions; "when an image comes back wrong, it is usually because one of them was left for the model to guess"; "Name the colours, not the feeling" with "charcoal grey" (not "scary colours"); `--no text` example.
- **Correction to the topic note:** "the more information you give it" is half right. Midjourney says short and clear prompts work best and long instructions can confuse it, but more of the right details gives more control. The video says "the right details, not more words".
- **B05 text** is quoted from my own GPS script (`youtube/how-gps-works/SCRIPT.md`, B02). The B06 prompt is written for this video and not yet run (no images, by choice); B08 says that's next week's test.
- **B08's "same seed"** follows last week's Midjourney Seeds video: a seed is a control for testing one change (Midjourney Seeds page).
